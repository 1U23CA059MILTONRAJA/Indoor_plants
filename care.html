How to Care for Indoor Plants

Indoor plants need proper care to grow healthy and beautiful. Follow these simple steps.

Plant Gallery

1. Watering

Give water when the soil becomes dry. Avoid giving too much water.

2. Sunlight

Place plants where they receive suitable sunlight.

3. Clean the Leaves

Clean leaves regularly to remove dust.

4. Good Soil

Use healthy soil with proper drainage.
